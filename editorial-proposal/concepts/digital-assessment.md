# Digital assessment platform — future concept ONLY
**NOT IMPLEMENTED · NOT AUTHORIZED FOR SCHOOL USE · 8.11.2026**

This is a separate potential product, not a feature of the document template or an endorsement by Deutsche Schule Mailand.

## Intended workflow
- Teacher creator prepares draft; optional editors may suggest changes.
- Authorized reviewer validates content, translations, permitted answer languages and learning accommodations.
- Release an immutable version with a stable document ID and content hash.
- Student has read-only questions and writable own answer area during an explicitly authorized session.
- Example only: 25 November 2026, launch window 06:00–18:00, preparation allowance 3 minutes, standard duration 60 minutes. To finish by 18:00 the latest launch would be 16:57 unless different rules explicitly permit extension.
- Server-owned deadline; save draft continuously; final submission seals answers; clear confirmation and recoverability/audit strategy.
- Exception path for technical outage, accessibility accommodations, additional authorized time and human supervisor override. A session must not be reset by refresh or multiple devices.
- Separate printed exam mode: download/print cannot be assumed to prevent work after time expires; paper has separate collection controls.

## Privacy and security
No camera access by default, no biometrics or automated behavioral inference. Remote proctoring involving minors demands a separate necessity/proportionality review, legal basis, DPIA where applicable, school approval, alternatives, retention and access controls. This proposal does not authorize it.

Minimize collection; don't publish live examination questions, results, student IDs, camera feeds or private URLs on GitHub. Accessible exam timer: minutes by default with warning at end, screen-reader announcements and no distracting seconds until genuinely needed.

## Out of scope
Implementation, remote identity proofing, storage, deployment, school integration, live proctoring and approval workflows. Those require separate design and explicit authorizations.
