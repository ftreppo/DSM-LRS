# Security and privacy
Public editorial proposal, **not** an operational school system.

- No private student/parent/teacher data, diagnoses, grades, photos, correspondence, student IDs, credentials, private links or non-public examination materials.
- Use synthetic samples. Redact any identifying or copyrighted source.
- Do not publish proprietary DSM logos or purport to speak officially for the school.
- Reference IDs and QR codes do not bypass access control.
- Camera proctoring, biometric analysis, live examination access and school authentication are **not implemented**.
- Any implementation involving minors requires institutional authorization, necessity/proportionality and a legal/privacy impact review.

For potentially sensitive reports, contact repository maintainer privately; do not disclose them in a public Issue.
