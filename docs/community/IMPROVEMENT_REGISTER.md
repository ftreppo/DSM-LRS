# Adapter Improvement Register

Structured register for evidence-backed improvement candidates emerging from Community Adapter Lab submissions.

## Status values

- `OBSERVED`
- `REPEATED_PATTERN`
- `PROPOSED`
- `TESTING`
- `ACCEPTED`
- `REJECTED`
- `RELEASED`

## Register

| ID | Pattern / hypothesis | Evidence | Classification | Status | Proposed change | Test/verification | Release |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | No community-derived improvement candidate yet | — | — | — | — | — | — |

## Promotion threshold

There is no fixed numeric threshold because severity matters.

Examples:
- one severe ASSESSMENT_SAFETY failure may justify immediate review;
- minor layout preferences normally require repeated evidence;
- repeated behavior across multiple subjects/languages is stronger evidence of a generic contract weakness.

Every ACCEPTED change must be traceable to:
1. evidence/submissions;
2. proposed change;
3. tests;
4. verification;
5. release/changelog entry.
