# C13 — DSM 2015 guideline: update now or wait for Länder legislation?

**Date:** 2026-10-13  
**Status:** DECISION STUDY / RECOMMEND ADDENDUM NOW  
**Scope:** DSM-local guideline governance; no individual accommodation decision.

## 1. New confirmed input

adopted by the Schulleitungskomitee on 12.02.2015 is **still in force at DSM**.

## 2. Current external authority

For Deutsche Schulen im Ausland, the most directly relevant current German authority is not a single Bundesland rule but the KMK/BLASchA Auslandsschul framework.

Current official sources include:

1. **Empfehlungen zur Förderung von Schülerinnen und Schülern mit besonderen Schwierigkeiten beim Lesen und Rechtschreiben sowie beim Rechnen an den Deutschen Schulen im Ausland**
   - BLASchA 17.03.2010
   - current version: **25.04.2025**

2. **Richtlinie für den abschlussbezogenen Nachteilsausgleich**
   - BLASchA **11.12.2024**

3. Current applicable examination rules, including DIA-PO / Rili DIA-PO where relevant.

The Deutsche Schule Mailand is a KMK-recognized Deutsche Auslandsschule. Its own public governance information identifies ZfA/KMK/BLASchA as the relevant German authorities for didactic/organizational matters and recognition of German qualifications.

## 3. Why waiting for one Bundesland is not the right primary gate

German Länder rules are useful comparative evidence, but they are not the primary authority layer for DSM as a Deutsche Auslandsschule.

The 2024 BLASchA guideline for graduation-related accommodations explicitly exists to create comparable treatment across the international examination regions despite differing Land-specific rules.

Therefore:

> a future or recent reform in one Bundesland may be informative, but it should not delay reconciliation with the already-current BLASchA 2024/2025 framework.

## 4. Relevant Länder development: Niedersachsen

Niedersachsen is a useful comparator because it has recently changed its school-law framework.

The Niedersächsisches Schulgesetz was amended in 2026. New § 58a establishes a statutory basis for Nachteilsausgleich and Notenschutz and expressly includes Lese-Rechtschreib-Störung and Rechenstörung among the possible conditions for Notenschutz under defined requirements.

Most of the school-law amendment entered into force on 01.08.2026.

A related implementing regulation on individual support, Nachteilsausgleich and Notenschutz went through consultation in 2026.

This reform is strategically interesting because it modernizes the distinction between:
- individual support;
- Nachteilsausgleich;
- Notenschutz.

However, it is a **Land-specific framework** and cannot simply be imported into DSM rules, especially where BLASchA rules for German qualifications and graduation-related assessment establish different boundaries.

## 5. Material gaps between DSM 2015 and current BLASchA framework

The 2015 DSM guideline remains locally operative, but several points should be reconciled against current authority.

### A. Source references

DSM 2015 is framed around the BLASchA 17.03.2010 decision and 2011 Italian law.

Current source references should explicitly include:
- BLASchA recommendations i.d.F. 25.04.2025;
- BLASchA Richtlinie abschlussbezogener Nachteilsausgleich 11.12.2024;
- current examination rules in their applicable version.

**Assessment:** UPDATE NOW.

### B. Förderplan review frequency

DSM 2015 states in the Gymnasium section that Förderpläne are generally revised every two years.

BLASchA 2025 states that measures must be reviewed during the process, time-limited and adjusted after renewed learning-status analysis; the Förderplan is continued annually when needed.

**Assessment:** POTENTIAL MATERIAL DRIFT — UPDATE NOW.

### C. Grade-based reduction of supports

DSM 2015 describes progressive reduction of several supports in grades 9–10 and expects some supports such as speech synthesis to be reduced except in severe cases.

Current BLASchA rules retain a developmental/fading objective but emphasize individualized, documented review and, for graduation-related accommodations, continuity with measures actually used before the final examination.

A support should therefore not disappear mechanically only because a pupil reaches a defined grade.

**Assessment:** REWORD / INDIVIDUALIZE NOW.

### D. Graduation-related Nachteilsausgleich

The 2024 BLASchA guideline now provides a specific formal framework.

Important current points include:
- professional/diagnostic basis plus pedagogical assessment;
- medical/external recommendations inform but do not automatically dictate the measure;
- no reduction of the fachlich-kognitive requirement;
- Notenschutz is excluded in the graduation-related framework;
- measures used in final examinations should correspond to measures already applied during the previous school years;
- specific application/approval procedure through the school and KMK-Beauftragte;
- current documentation requirements.

**Assessment:** ADD EXPLICIT CROSS-REFERENCE NOW rather than maintain a parallel DSM interpretation.

### E. Time allowances / tools

DSM 2015 contains local examples including up to 30% extra time in some grade bands.

The 2024 graduation-related BLASchA guideline contains more specific ceilings/examples, including:
- general exam-time extension up to 25% in the listed framework;
- spelling-related extension up to 20%, normally max 10% in mathematics;
- reading-related extension up to 25%;
- technical and layout aids subject to the assessed competence and individual decision.

These graduation-related values should not be overridden by older local wording.

**Assessment:** UPDATE NOW for Abschluss-/central-exam context; ordinary classroom arrangements remain individual and context-dependent.

### F. Documentation and provenance

Current BLASchA texts place stronger emphasis on documented Fördermaßnahmen, current diagnostic/support evidence, prior use and formal approval.

This aligns with the DSM-LRS architecture already adopted:
source → teacher observation → approved school plan → approved Operational Profile → scoped derivative.

**Assessment:** UPDATE NOW / LOW CONFLICT.

## 6. Recommended decision

### Do not perform a full rewrite immediately.

Instead use a two-stage approach.

### Stage A — 2026 Addendum / controlled update — NOW

Create a short DSM-local addendum or revision note that:

1. confirms the 12.02.2015 document remains the local base document;
2. replaces static source references with current-version references;
3. states that current BLASchA/KMK rules prevail where newer or more specific;
4. updates Förderplan review semantics;
5. removes any impression of automatic grade-based withdrawal of support;
6. incorporates/cross-references the 2024 graduation-related NTA procedure;
7. resolves old time-limit/tool wording for central/final examinations;
8. preserves the Italian-law/local bilingual-school elements that remain DSM-specific;
9. defines an annual source-review trigger.

This is the minimum safe change.

### Stage B — Full DSM guideline v2 — LATER

Prepare a complete rewrite only after enough evidence accumulates, for example:
- the Niedersachsen implementation framework is final and useful as comparative evidence;
- other Länder converge on materially new concepts;
- Italian rules change;
- DSM's own operational process changes;
- accumulated Addenda make the 2015 structure difficult to maintain.

## 7. Länder watch policy

Länder developments should be monitored as **comparative evidence**, not imported as authority by default.

Priority comparative watch:
1. Niedersachsen — new § 58a + implementing regulation;
2. Schleswig-Holstein — established Nachteilsausgleich / Notenschutz separation;
3. Bayern — mature BaySchO LRS structure;
4. Berlin — LRS/NTA/Notenschutz documentation and review practice.

Promotion rule:

`Land rule → comparative evidence → BLASchA compatibility check → DSM decision`

Never:

`Land rule → automatic DSM rule`

## 8. Decision recommendation

**RECOMMENDATION: ADDENDUM NOW / FULL REWRITE LATER.**

Waiting for a Bundesland reform before doing anything is not recommended because the most relevant Auslandsschule authority has already changed in 2024/2025.

A future Länder reform may improve the full DSM v2 design, but should not postpone correction of known source/version and procedural drift.
